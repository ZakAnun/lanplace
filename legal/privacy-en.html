BlueScopes Privacy Policy

Developer: Li Minglin
Contact: linhenji@163.com
Effective date: 2026-10-25

1. Introduction

BlueScopes is provided by developer Li Minglin. This app has no accounts and no developer backend. Scan and discovery results are not uploaded to the developer.

2. Information we process

2.1 Bluetooth

When you use Nearby device discovery, this app shows nearby Bluetooth identifiers, names, signal strength, connectable state, and advertisement summaries on this device. This works indoors, outdoors, and in any setting.

2.2 Local network (mDNS)

When you are connected to Wi-Fi, this app uses system mDNS to find services advertised on that LAN and shows the service name, type, IP, and port on this device after they resolve. It does not scan the whole subnet and cannot list every device on the router. Client isolation on public Wi-Fi may limit discovery.

2.3 Wi-Fi

This app may read the name (SSID) of the Wi-Fi you are connected to and show it on the discovery page, for display only. It is not uploaded.

2.4 Storage

Scan results are not persisted by default. Closing or uninstalling the app removes them. The emulator or demo mode may show simulated devices.

You can open this policy in the system browser from the in-app link. There is no third-party analytics or advertising SDK.

3. Permissions

PermissionPurpose
Nearby devices / BluetoothScan and display nearby Bluetooth devices
InternetDiscover LAN services on the current Wi-Fi via system mDNS; data is not uploaded to the developer

You can manage these permissions in system settings. Denying a permission may disable the matching feature.

4. Third-party SDKs

This app does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

5. Updates

We may update this policy. Material changes will be noted in the app or in release notes. The text on this page is authoritative.
